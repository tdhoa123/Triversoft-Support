Frequently Asked Questions

The camera does not open or the screen is black.

Go to Settings > Manual Tracking: Camera FX and make sure Camera access is turned on. Then fully close the app and open it again.

How do I use Finger Lens?

Hold up both hands and use your thumbs and index fingers to form a four-point frame. The selected effect appears inside the frame. Move your hands to reposition it.

The finger frame is not detected.

Make sure you are in a well-lit area, both hands are fully visible in the camera view, and your thumbs and index fingers are clearly separated. Avoid cluttered backgrounds and move your hands slowly.

What is Reverse Filter?

Reverse Filter keeps the area inside your finger frame natural and applies the selected effect to everything outside of it.

How does Beat Sync work?

Beat Sync changes filters in time with the beat of the music while you record, so your short videos feel more dynamic.

Where are my photos and videos saved?

Captured photos and videos are saved to your Photos library. If nothing is saved, check Settings > Manual Tracking: Camera FX > Photos and allow access.

The app feels slow or the device gets warm.

Real-time effects and hand tracking use the camera and processor heavily. Try closing other apps, lowering video quality if available, and updating to the latest iOS version.
